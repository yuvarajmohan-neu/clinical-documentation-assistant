Check a note

Paste a finished note that was written somewhere else. The assistant checks it against Article 31 requirements and tells you what to fix. It doesn’t rewrite your note.

With a date, the check can confirm the note was finished on time.

What gets checked

  • Required fields and timelines from the Article 31 rule set, e.g. signature, date, duration, service type
  • Medicaid and NYS documentation standards that apply to every program
  • Clinical content: intervention and client response, measurable progress on goals, medical necessity

Each finding links to the regulation section it comes from.
